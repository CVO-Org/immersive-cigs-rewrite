# Regional brand picks (research for issue #191)

Status 2026-11-25: all seven recommended picks are in the mod (Ducados, Klubi, John Silver, Parisienne, Gudang Garam, Pielroja, Cleopatra), and the terrains below have terrain presets. The runner-ups (the "b" and "c" candidates of each region) are not implemented and have no issue: they are the starting point for a second brand in a region.

## How to read this

- **Checked** = confirmed by a web search this session (sources at the end). **Memory** = from general knowledge, not verified. Pack looks marked "memory" must be checked against a reference photo before anyone draws the pack.
- No direct image URLs were retrieved. The Wikipedia article of each brand is given as the starting point (the infobox usually carries a pack photo); Stanford's tobacco ad archive (https://tobacco.stanford.edu) and Wikimedia Commons are the next stops. Treat every "pack look" line as a pointer, not a spec.
- Brands already in the mod or with an issue (not proposed again): Marlboro, Camel, Morley, NIL, Prince, Matelot, Jaffudi, Zargabad, Sharig, Casa Sports, Hwarang, Ruby Queen, Gudok, Zamok, Sosna; open issues Newport #357, Belomorkanal #358, Parliament #359, Lambert & Butler #360, Drina #361, Sportsman #362, Black Death #363, Sobranie Black Russian #364, Virginia Slims #365.
- Model fit: the mod has filter and filterless models. Cork-tip, slim, papirosa and cigarillo models do not exist, so a candidate that needs one is marked "new model".
- Era rule: WW2 looks only go in 1940s presets. Everything below is post-war or timeless.

## 1. Sahrani (Spanish/Atlantic): `sara`, `saralite`, `sara_dbe1`, `porto`, `intro`, `Sehreno`

### 1a. Ducados (best)
- **Country, years:** Spain, 1963 to today (Tabacalera, now Altadis/Imperial). Checked.
- **Pack look:** The original "Ducados Negro" is the dark, strong black-tobacco cigarette; the name and pack refer to the gold ducat coin (checked). Dark pack with gold lettering and a coin motif (memory). The lighter "Rubio" is a different, pale pack, so stick to the Negro look.
- **Reference:** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ducados_(cigarette)
- **Filter:** filter, fits the current models.
- **Fit:** The single most recognisably Spanish cigarette. Fits `sara*`, Rahmadi and Sehreno in the 1970s to modern range, which suits the Cold War and modern settings of Sahrani.
- **Concerns:** The coin logo is simple, so low risk. 1963 start means no WW2 or early postwar presets (not an issue for Sahrani).

### 1b. Celtas
- **Country, years:** Spain, 1930s onwards (memory, check start year), filterless "Celtas Cortos". 
- **Pack look:** Blue pack with simple lettering (memory, verify).
- **Filter:** filterless, fits the filterless model.
- **Fit:** The Franco-era working man's smoke. Good if a gritty older look is wanted for Sahrani's civil-war flavour (`sara_dbe1`).
- **Concerns:** Less well known abroad than Ducados; pack details unverified.

### 1c. Fortuna
- **Country, years:** Spain, 1943 to today (Tabacalera, listed among its brands, checked; launch year memory). 
- **Pack look:** Plain pack with large lettering (memory, verify).
- **Filter:** filter.
- **Concerns:** Very similar slot to Ducados but less iconic. Only worthwhile as a second Spanish brand.

**Recommendation: Ducados.** Celtas as a later filterless addition.

## 2. Finland: `ruha`, `vt7`, `Maksniemi`

### 2a. Klubi (best)
- **Country, years:** Finland, made by Rettig from 1901; the "Klubi 7" variant took off in 1907; bought by Altadis in 2003; the "Klubi 77" revival failed; discontinued around the mid-2000s. Checked.
- **Pack look:** Classic old-style Finnish pack with the word "Klubi" in a serif or script (memory, verify). Reference: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Klubi
- **Filter:** Started as a plain cigarette; filtered versions followed in the postwar decades (memory). Either model fits.
- **Fit:** The classic Finnish cigarette, present through the Winter War era to the 2000s. Fits all three terrains and any era from the Cold War to modern.
- **Concerns:** Pack details unverified. Keep the pack look to the 1970s to 90s.

### 2b. Työmies ("Workman")
- **Country, years:** Finland, Tupakkatehdas Fennia, 1902 to 1984, filterless. Checked.
- **Pack look:** Unknown, to be looked up.
- **Fit:** Strong working-class Cold War Finland brand; filterless fits the existing model.
- **Concerns:** Ends in 1984, so no modern presets.

### 2c. Colt
- **Country, years:** Finland, 1964 on; once the best-seller until Marlboro and L&M overtook it. Checked. Merged with Rettig-Strengberg in 1976.
- **Fit:** Filter, 1960s to 2000s. Good second pick; less folkloric.

**Recommendation: Klubi.** Työmies as the filterless follow-up.

## 3. Sweden / Norway: `stubbhult`, `archie_w`, `mehland`, `jns_tria`

### 3a. John Silver (best, Sweden)
- **Country, years:** Sweden, launched 1947 by the state tobacco monopoly (Svenska Tobaksmonopolet), the first "American blend" by a Swedish maker; filterless. Checked.
- **Pack look:** Pirate theme from the Long John Silver name (memory, verify). Reference: https://www.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Silver_(cigarette)
- **Filter:** filterless, fits.
- **Fit:** Iconic Swedish brand across the Cold War. Fits `stubbhult`; passable for Norway/fictional Nordic maps.
- **Concerns:** Pirate illustration may be fiddly to reproduce. Discontinuation date unchecked; keep to the 1950s to 80s look.

### 3b. Teddy (Norway)
- **Country, years:** Norway, Tiedemanns, launched 1914, named after Theodore Roosevelt, Virginia tobacco, discontinued 2010. Checked. Reference: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Teddy_(cigarette)
- **Fit:** The Norwegian pick if the maps are split; pack look not checked.
- **Concerns:** Roosevelt/teddy imagery to be verified.

### 3c. Blend
- **Country, years:** Sweden, introduced 1971 by Svenska Tobaks, lower-tar. Checked (one source). Filter. Reference: https://en.wikipedia-on-ipfs.org/wiki/Blend_(cigarette)
- **Concerns:** A fine modern Swedish filler, but a generic name.

**Recommendation: John Silver for both** (one brand, one PR). Add Teddy only if Norway should differ from Sweden. Note the existing Prince (Danish) stand-in is a reasonable fallback for Norway.

## 4. Switzerland: `Napf`, `NapfWinter`

### 4a. Parisienne (best)
- **Country, years:** Switzerland, from 1887, Burrus family (Boncourt, Jura); filtered versions from 1947; owned by Rothmans (1996) then BAT (1999 or 2000, sources differ); still made in Boncourt. Checked. Reference: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parisienne_(cigarette)
- **Pack look:** Memory only: a coloured pack with the name in a flowing script. Verify.
- **Filter:** filter, fits. Era: 1947 on, fine for Cold War and modern.
- **Concerns:** Obscure outside Switzerland, but a genuine national brand.

### 4b. Brunette
- Swiss filterless brand of the Burrus group (memory, unverified start year). Candidate if a filterless Swiss brand is wanted. Verify before issue.

### 4c. Brissago / Villiger (cigars)
- Villiger is a Swiss cigar maker (1888, Pfäffikon). Checked. Cigars are officer-only at unit class level, so not for terrain presets.

**Recommendation: Parisienne.** This replaces the NIL stand-in with a real local brand.

## 5. Southeast Asia, modern: `pulau`, `rhspkl` (Prei Khmaoch Luong)

### 5a. Gudang Garam (best)
- **Country, years:** Indonesia, kretek (clove) maker; the company dates to 1958 (memory for year); hand-rolled origins, "salt warehouse" name; about 20% market share, second largest in Indonesia. Checked (company facts), year from memory. Reference: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gudang_Garam
- **Pack look:** Red filter pack, white and gold lettering, a building illustration (memory, verify).
- **Filter:** filter. Smoke is clove, which the mod does not model; fine.
- **Fit:** Defines Southeast Asian smoking, widely exported; right for a modern setting.
- **Concerns:** The warehouse illustration is detailed. Check trademark/logo reproduction.

### 5b. Dji Sam Soe (234)
- **Country, years:** Indonesia, Sampoerna, 1913 (Liem Seeng Tee, Surabaya), filterless kretek, "Two Three Four" in Hokkien, long packs of ten rows of two. Checked. Reference: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dji_Sam_Soe
- **Fit:** Filterless fits the existing model. Gold-black-red pack, 234 numerals (memory).
- **Concerns:** Longer pack shape than the mod's pack model; the Sampoerna logo is intricate.

### 5c. ARA (Cambodia)
- **Country, years:** BAT Cambodia, sold since 1996; BAT holds about 40% of the market. Checked. Hero and Angkor are also sold there; Fine (Imperial/Huotraco) and 555 are other popular names.
- **Fit:** The truest local pick for Prei Khmaoch Luong.
- **Concerns:** Obscure, little reference material found, recent brand. Only if a Cambodia-specific brand is wanted.

**Recommendation: Gudang Garam.**

## 6. South America: `lingor3`, `dingor`, `UMB_Colombia`

### 6a. Pielroja (best)
- **Country, years:** Colombia, first sold in Medellín in 1924; Coltabaco was bought by Philip Morris in 2005; the plants closed in 2019 and the brand ended. Checked. Filterless in its classic form (memory).
- **Pack look:** Red-and-white pack with the word "Pielroja" and a native-American profile (memory, verify).
- **Filter:** filterless, fits the existing model.
- **Fit:** The iconic Colombian cigarette; matches `UMB_Colombia` exactly and works for the jungle islands.
- **Concerns:** The native imagery is a possible sensitivity; the lettering alone could carry it. Needs a decision from the owner.

### 6b. Hollywood (Brazil)
- **Country, years:** Brazil, Souza Cruz, introduced 1931, now BAT; discontinued in 2020 according to Wikipedia (conflicting; check). Checked. Reference: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hollywood_(cigarette)
- **Fit:** Wide continental recognition. Colour and logo to verify.

### 6c. Argentine brands
- Jockey Club and Derby (Nobleza Piccardo) were dropped for Pall Mall; Nobleza Piccardo formed in 1978. Checked. Years of the brands themselves unchecked. Also Massalin's Particulares. Weaker fit for the island terrains.

**Recommendation: Pielroja.**

## 7. Middle East / Arab world: `pja310` (Al Rayak), `juju_sahatra` (Sa'hatra)

### 7a. Cleopatra (best)
- **Country, years:** Egypt, Eastern Company (founded 1920), Cleopatra introduced 1961; the most sold cigarette in Egypt, sold in most African and some Middle Eastern and Asian countries. Checked. Reference: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cleopatra_(cigarette)
- **Pack look:** A profile of Cleopatra and Egyptian lettering (memory, verify).
- **Filter:** filter, fits. Era: 1961 on, fits Cold War and modern.
- **Fit:** The best-known Arab-world cigarette, so a genuine alternative to the Takistan stand-ins for Arab maps.
- **Concerns:** Egyptian rather than Gulf or Levant; tiny risk of mismatching the fictional Al Rayak and Sa'hatra.

### 7b. Cedars / Hamra (Levant)
- Cedars (Lebanon) and Al-Hamra (Syria) are national brands (memory only, nothing confirmed by search). Verify the names, years and packs before proposing.

**Recommendation: Cleopatra.**
