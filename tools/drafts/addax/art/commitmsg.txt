Add Addax cigarettes

Addax is a mainstream American Turkish-blend filter cigarette for
international factions (IDAP, ION, UN) and as general filler for
modern western factions. Copied from voron: 20 cigarettes, 17 puffs,
five flavour lines. Pack faces and paper logo are a first pass drawn
as SVG (ivory card, deep teal, copper, a navy addax head with spiral
horns before a desert sun).
